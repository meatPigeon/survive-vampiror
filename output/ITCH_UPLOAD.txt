Command a zombie horde against one knight across three escalating waves.
Survive the halberd, piercing crossbow and mounted stampede. Recruit temporary
zombies and choose new perks between waves. Keep your permanent horde alive.

Newbie: reduced damage; crossbow bolts are still lethal.
Normal: every knight hit kills a zombie.
