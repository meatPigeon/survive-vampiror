# Simplified toy-style character prompts — v4

Created with the built-in image generation tool as edits of the v3 concepts. Direction: chunky primitive forms, broad color blocks, simple cartoon faces and reduced surface detail. Previous concepts preserved.

## zombie-v4.png

Reference: /home/meatpigeon/Projects/survive-vampiror/art/concepts/zombie-v3.png

Use case: style-transfer. Substantially art-direct and simplify the attached chubby character into a charming, highly stylized indie-game collectible toy. This is a major shape-language simplification, NOT just a texture cleanup. Keep the character recognizable and chubby but rebuild it from about a dozen bold primitive-like masses: one big bean-shaped belly, squat short legs, stout capsule arms, oversized simple head, mitten-shaped hands with a thumb and two broad finger lobes, chunky single-piece shoes. Approximately 3.5 heads tall. Broad softened polygon faces and clean bevels, low-poly-friendly construction with simple flat color materials, a few broad soft lighting gradients. Surfaces COMPLETELY clean and untextured: no pores, grain, scratches, patina, dirt, fabric weave, realistic wrinkles, anatomical modeling, fingernails, knuckles, hair strands or tiny hardware. Face must be an extremely simple playful cartoon: two asymmetrical white eyeballs with black dot pupils, tiny minimal nose, one simple dark mouth shape, at most two block-shaped teeth. No detailed eye sockets, eyelid folds or skin creases. Clear readable silhouette that works at small game-camera size. Keep the original main costume color blocks and comical off-balance high-step pose, adapt to the shorter proportions. One full-body character centered in a square frame with comfortable empty margins, three-quarter view, seamless flat warm golden-yellow background, soft studio illumination and a single subtle ground shadow. A cohesive designer-vinyl-toy look rendered in 3D, expressive and slightly silly. No environment, props beyond the specified costume, labels, text or watermark. Character: plump goofy olive-green zombie. A big lopsided rounded head with TWO simple chunky dark hair tufts, blank googly eyes and one square tooth. Pale cream shirt wrapped over a very round belly, with only TWO large triangular notches at its hem, no additional tears or stains. Short terracotta-orange trousers with a clean simple hem, rounded dark brown shoes. Reduce the mint mask beneath the chin to a single smooth mint crescent with two simple straps, no pleats or seams. Simplify rather than add anything. Push friendly grotesque cartoon appeal, no gore.
